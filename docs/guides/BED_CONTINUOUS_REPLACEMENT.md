# Continuous bed-background replacement experiment

Status: **unapproved**. The original 5k scene and all previous previews remain unchanged.

The new workflow uses deleted source IDs only for provenance and the rough edit extent. `build_continuous_background.py` intersects accepted bed-mask camera rays with independently fitted wall/floor planes, rejects invalid intersections, extends the patch beyond the silhouette, and makes surface-aligned Gaussians from visible wall/carpet donors. It records removed Gaussian records, IDs, positions, per-view mask references, and rendered deletion footprints separately from replacement geometry. New carpet features come from carpet donors; new wall features come from wall donors. The 128D PLY schema is preserved.

`protect_wooden_frame.py` creates an independent frame mask and protected source-ID list. `collect_footprint_candidates.py` finds broad splats overlapping the bed mask even when their centers are outside it. `select_depth_occluders.py` optionally suppresses those whose centers lie in front of the fitted background in multiple views, excluding protected frame IDs. `optimize_continuous_background.py` optimizes only added Gaussian appearance/opacity and mixed boundary opacity, with original photos used outside the bed masks and a shared background target inside them; unrelated original splats and semantic features are frozen. Every stage creates a new directory and refuses to overwrite one.

## Results on this capture

The experimental continuous preview is `output/bed-ops/bed-continuous-background-v2/candidate.ply` (6,302 floor and 8,457 wall Gaussians added). The local optimizer preview is `output/bed-ops/bed-continuous-optimized-v1/candidate.ply` (56.5 s, 3,691 MiB peak process RAM, 1,290 MiB peak GPU allocation). The depth-occluder preview is `output/bed-ops/bed-depth-occluders-v1/candidate.ply` (1,501 more splats suppressed; 11.2 s, 3,863 MiB RAM, 724 MiB GPU). The original 6,788 removed IDs and 2,231 attenuated IDs are saved in `bed-continuous-background-v2/deletion-provenance.npz`; per-view removed/attenuated footprints are in `bed-deletion-footprints-v1`.

None passes visual acceptance at held-out `frame_0134`, `frame_0141`, and `frame_0143`. The depth preview retains a broad flat brown, bed-shaped region and a dark wall/floor gap; it also changes pixels outside the edit mask and in the independently masked frame. The overhead synthetic camera lacks reliable source coverage, so it cannot provide the requested acceptance view. The wall plane fit has only 16.8% inliers (q90 error 0.263), wall color samples are far from the synthesized patch (q90 distance 3.83 scene units), and carpet donor q90 distance is 2.20. These are extrapolations, not recovered hidden observations. Local color/opacity optimization did not fix the geometry or the missing wall appearance.

Current defaults require at least 30% wall-plane inliers, wall sample q90 distance at most 1.5, and carpet donor q90 distance at most 1.5; this capture fails those checks. The experimental previews were generated with explicit weaker thresholds solely for diagnosis. Do not promote any of them or claim a finished bed replacement. Better coverage of the wall and carpet behind the bed, or a separately reviewed generated background with multi-view depth consistency, is needed to make an approvable repair.

Validation reports: `bed-continuous-evaluation-frame-v1/report.json` and `bed-depth-evaluation-v1/report.json`. The full Python test suite passes 60 tests. Numerical schema/plane checks do not constitute visual approval.
